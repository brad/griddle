How to play

Find six overlapping five-letter words. Each guess is scored against all six words at once.

  • You have 10 guesses. Six is the theoretical minimum.
  • Green means the letter is correct in that square and remains locked.
  • Yellow means the selected guess contains a letter that belongs elsewhere in one or more words through that square.
  • Yellow hints belong only to the selected numbered guess. Select an earlier number to compare its clues.
  • Where a row and column cross and both words hint at the square, both letters are shown — the row word's hint on top, the column word's below.
  • Duplicate letters: once a target word's copy of a letter is green or yellow, it cannot make a second copy yellow.
  • Use the built-in keyboard—there is no mobile text field.
Example: Tap 1 and 2 to see clues for each guess — guess 1 has a square showing two hints
